WHAT GETS EVALUATED

① Action Verb — Does it start strong? (Built, Analyzed, Modeled — not "Helped" or "Assisted")
② Quantification — Is there a number? ($, %, x multiple, basis points)
③ Finance Signal — Does it use IB vocabulary correctly? (WACC, EV, MOIC, DCF, etc.)
④ Specificity — Is it tied to a real company, deal, or output?
⑤ Conciseness — Under 2 lines? Does every word earn its place?
